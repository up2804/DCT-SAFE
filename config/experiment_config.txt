DCT-SAFE reproducibility configuration
=====================================

Synthetic cyber twin
- Features: packet_rate, mean_packet_size, flow_duration, connection_rate,
  failed_connections, byte_rate, inter_arrival_mean, unique_destinations
- Train stream: 1500 benign + 1500 DoS
- Test stream: 5000 flows
- Attack start: flow 2000
- Window: 250 flows
- Scenarios: static, gradual, abrupt, oscillating, stealth, hybrid
- Seeds: 42, 123, 2024, 3407, 7777
- Feedback contamination: 0%, 40%
- Feedback delay: 1, 5 windows
- Persistence: 2 consecutive drift-positive windows
- Cooldown: 2 windows
- Primary learner: standardized Logistic Regression

CIC-IDS2017 external protocol
- Initial training: Monday + Tuesday
- Future stream: remaining CIC-IDS2017 files in temporal order
- Window: 250 flows
- Seeds: 42, 123, 2024, 3407, 7777
- Contamination: 0%, 40%
- Delay: 1, 5 windows
- Family-level logging: static/adaptive F1 and recovery fields

Important
- The fresh CIC family-recovery results are not claimed to be an exact reproduction
  of the historical aggregate CIC pipeline.
- Incomplete matched Random Forest runs are not used for manuscript claims.
- The CIC family analysis is interpreted cautiously because future attack families
  may be unseen in the initial training period.
